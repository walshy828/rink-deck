      Ref quick reference

      Drawn as the referee faces you; arrows show the motion. Signals tagged check are from memory of the usual USA Hockey / NHL-style signals and should be confirmed against the current USA Hockey officiating rulebook or your supervisor.

      Penalty times (EHF, 15-minute periods)

      Minor1:30
      • Team plays shorthanded
      • A goal by the other team ends the first minor being served
      • Player sits in the box
      Major5:00
      • Team shorthanded; served in full, a goal does not end it
      • Every major also carries a game misconduct (player ejected, a teammate serves the 5:00)
      Misconduct10:00
      • Team is not shorthanded; a sub goes on right away
      • Player sits 10:00, then stays on the bench until the next stoppage
      • With a minor: the minor is served first, the 10:00 starts when it ends
      • Second misconduct in a game becomes a game misconduct
      Game misconductOut
      • Out for the rest of the game, plus the entire next scheduled game (EHF)
      • Shorthanded only if paired with a minor or major
      Match5:00
      • Team shorthanded; not ended by a goal
      • Ejected; suspended until the Referee-in-Chief reviews
      • For attempting to injure, spearing and similar

      Good to know

      • Minors are prorated by period length: over 12 and under 17 minutes = 1:30. A double minor is 3:00.
      • Goalie penalties are served by a teammate in the box.

      When players come back

      • Matching (coincident) penalties: equal penalties to both teams, such as a minor each. Both players serve their time but neither team is shorthanded. They return at the first whistle after their time is up.
      • Misconduct: the player returns at the first whistle after the 10:00 is up.
      • Use the Calc tab to work out the clock time and whether to wait for the whistle.
      The whistle rule for matching penalties is common practice, not stated on the EHF page. Confirm with the referee.
      Source: EHF page (last updated 2/3/23) plus secondary USA Hockey summaries. Serving rules are very likely right but not checked against the rulebook.

      Common Midget infractions

      Minor, or major + game misconduct if injury / excessive
      • Hooking, holding, tripping
      • Slashing, high-sticking
      • Elbowing, cross-checking
      • Interference
      Minor + misconduct, or major + game misconduct if serious
      • Boarding, charging
      • Checking from behind
      • Head contact
      Fighting
      • Major plus game misconduct
      Match penalties
      • Attempt to injure, spearing and similar
      • Mandatory suspension review

      EHF game rules

      Clock and score
      • Stop time, 15 / 15 / 15 for Midgets (the page's "two halves" text contradicts its own table)
      • Lead of 7 goals in the 3rd: clock runs until the lead is 5 or less, unless both coaches agree otherwise
      • Recorded to a maximum 10-goal differential; stats stop beyond that
      • Not stated whether the penalty clock runs during the 7-goal running clock: ask a ref
      Time-outs and overtime
      • No time-outs in the regular season; one per team in championship games
      • Playoff OT: one 5:00 4v4 sudden death, then 5:00 3v3 periods until someone scores; teams don't switch ends
      Bench, officials and eligibility
      • Max 4 coaches, all USA Hockey certified (violation: 3-game suspension for the responsible official)
      • Pulling a team off the ice: automatic one-year suspension for the coach
      • Playoffs: rostered by Dec 15 and played at least 70% of regular-season games
      • The referee's call on facts is final

      Scorekeeper tips

      On every penalty
      • Record player number, infraction, length, and time on the clock
      • Know whether the team is shorthanded before you start the penalty clock
      • Combined minor + misconduct: time the minor first
      • If unsure, ask the referee at the next stoppage
      Not an official rulebook. Where this differs from the current USA Hockey rulebook or your league, the rulebook and the referee win.
